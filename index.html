Good to know
  • The first visit takes about 20 seconds while the Excel engine loads.
  • Very large files work but are slower: expect roughly 20,000 rows per 45 seconds.
  • Excel allows at most 1,048,575 rows per sheet; anything beyond is cut and you will be told.
  • Output columns follow the order they have in your source file.
